Bring your own copy of SIMTOWER.EXE — or the compressed SIMTOWER.EX_ straight off the CD; this page expands it. It is parsed entirely in your browser to load the game's art and sound — there is no server, and the file never leaves your machine.

Waiting for your EXE…
Where do I find SIMTOWER.EXE?
  • It's the game's main program file — named SIMTOWER.EXE, about 6 MB, found in an installed SIMTOWER folder.
  • Only have the CD (or a CD image)? The disc carries a compressed copy named SIMTOWER.EX_ (note the underscore) — drop that here as-is; this page expands it for you. Open a CD image with 7-Zip or any unarchiver to get at it. No installer needed.
  • Floppy disks? Disks 2 and 3 hold SIMTOWER.E1_ and SIMTOWER.E2_ — select the two together and drop both at once.
  • No disc? SimTower hasn't been sold in decades; copies circulate on game-preservation sites (a web search for “SimTower Windows download” gets you there). Downloads are usually a ZIP or CD image — look inside for SIMTOWER.EXE or SIMTOWER.EX_.
  • All known US releases work (1.0 and 1.1b). The Macintosh version won't — it stores its art differently. Windows version only.
  • Picked the wrong file? No harm — this page checks the file's structure and tells you what it got instead.

Your EXE and saves live in this browser's local storage only — upload once, it's remembered here. Sound starts with your first click (browser rule, not ours).
